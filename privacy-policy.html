Privacy Policy

  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob — banner ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads, and optional rewarded video ads — which can collect an advertising ID and device information as described below.
  • If you're in the EU or UK, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form asks for your permission before any personalized ads are shown.
  • Real-money purchases are not available yet. If we add them in the future, this policy will be updated first.
  • We never sell your data, and the app is not directed at children.

1. Introduction

GridCascade is a block-puzzle game developed by erakarstudio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects when you play, why, and what choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

None. GridCascade doesn't have an account system, a login, or a profile — there is nothing to sign up for, so we never ask for your name, email address, or any contact detail.

Information collected automatically, via Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. While an ad is loading or being shown, AdMob can automatically collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent device identifier) Showing and, where permitted, personalizing ads
Device information (model, OS version, screen size, language) Serving ads correctly sized and formatted for your device
Approximate location (derived from your IP address) Serving regionally relevant ads and required compliance (e.g. EU/UK consent)
Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, whether a rewarded video finished) Measuring ad performance and unlocking rewarded-ad bonuses

All data AdMob collects travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We don't operate our own servers that store this data — it lives in Google's own systems, governed by Google's privacy policy (linked in Section 6).

3. Data stored only on your device

Everything about how you play — your score, coin and gem balances, unlocked themes, settings (music/sound/vibration/language), daily-streak progress, and current board state — is saved locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. We do not run a game server, we do not have a database of player accounts, and none of this data is ever transmitted to us. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, this data is gone for good — we have no copy of it anywhere.

4. Advertising & your choices

GridCascade shows three kinds of ads through Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads — a small ad strip anchored to the bottom of the screen while you play.
  • Interstitial ads — occasional full-screen ads shown between games.
  • Rewarded video ads — optional ads you choose to watch yourself, in exchange for bonus coins, gems, or continuing a finished game.

If you're in the EU or UK, the app shows a consent form the first time you open it, through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and will not show personalized ads until you respond. You can change your ad-consent choice later from the app's Settings.

On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads — this is a device-level setting that applies to every app, not just GridCascade.

5. In-app purchases

GridCascade does not currently offer any real-money purchases. All in-game currency (coins and gems) is earned by playing or by watching optional rewarded ads — nothing in the app is bought with real money today. If we introduce real-money purchases in the future (for example, a "Remove Ads" option or currency packs), we will update this policy to describe exactly what payment and purchase-history data is involved before that feature goes live.

6. Third-party services

GridCascade is built with Capacitor and currently integrates one third-party service that can collect data, described above:

  • Google Privacy Policy (covers Google AdMob)

The app also bundles the Firebase Analytics and RevenueCat SDKs for potential future use (basic gameplay analytics and purchase handling, respectively), but as of the date above neither is configured or actively collecting data. If that changes, this policy will be updated and the relevant provider's own privacy policy will be linked here.

7. Data sharing & sale

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with anyone except Google AdMob as described above, strictly so it can serve ads — never for unrelated purposes.

8. Data retention

Gameplay progress (score, coins, gems, settings) is stored locally on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data — we hold no copy. Data collected by AdMob is retained according to Google's own retention schedule, described in the privacy policy linked in Section 6.

9. Children's privacy

GridCascade is offered to a general audience and is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner not permitted by applicable law, and ads are not configured to specifically target children.

10. Your rights & choices

Because GridCascade has no account system, there is no personal profile of you for us to access, correct, or delete on our end — your gameplay data lives only on your own device, under your own control (see Section 3). If you have a question about advertising data specifically, you can also exercise your choices directly with Google (Section 4) or reach out to us using the contact details below and we'll help however we can.

11. Data security

We rely on the security practices of Google (AdMob), which uses encrypted connections and industry-standard safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work only with an established provider that takes this seriously.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it — including if we introduce real-money purchases or activate additional analytics — we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page first. Significant changes will also be reflected in the app's Google Play store listing.
